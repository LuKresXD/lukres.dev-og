AutoStickers

A high-speed Telegram bot that automatically purchased new sticker packs the moment they dropped — JWT-authenticated sessions and a custom checkout flow built for speed.

Ran on a 20% commission model: over $152k in total purchase volume across ~2,300 users, with the Telegram Bot API driving user flows and a dedicated database for users, orders, and accounting.

Archived after the store moved to randomized allocation, which made instant purchases infeasible.
